description: How to power a GEOSTIX on and off using a magnet, without the base socket.

When the base socket is not available, the GEOSTIX can be powered on and off with a simple magnet, by holding it near the **charge indicator** on the rear of the device.

<Aside type="tip" title="Locate the charge indicator">
The charge indicator is the small green LED on the rear panel, next to the GEOSTIX logo. See [LEDs, Status Indicators](/en/geostix/leds/) for reference.
</Aside>

<Aside type="tip" title="No magnet at hand?">
The magnetic tip of the GEOSTIX charging cable can be used as a magnet for this purpose.
</Aside>

## Power on

<Steps>

1. Hold the magnet next to the charge indicator for a moment. The device powers on.

</Steps>

## Power off

Powering off requires a short two-pass sequence with the magnet:

<Steps>

1. **First pass:** bring the magnet close to the charge indicator, then move it away.
2. **Second pass:** bring the magnet close again, and hold it there for **at least 1 second**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="Timing matters">
The whole sequence (first pass, removal, and second pass) must be completed within **5 seconds**. If too much time elapses between the two passes, the device will not power off and the sequence must be restarted.
</Aside>
